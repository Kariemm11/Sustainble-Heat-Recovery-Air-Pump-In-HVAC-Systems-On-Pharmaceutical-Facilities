01 — The challenge

Pharmaceutical air is expensive air.

GMP production areas need clean, tightly controlled, often once-through air. Every cubic metre is filtered, heated or cooled, then exhausted — taking its conditioning energy with it.

High air-change rates

Cleanrooms are flushed many times per hour to dilute and remove particles, so fans and coils move far more air than in ordinary buildings.

Large outdoor-air fractions

To avoid cross-contamination much of the return air cannot be recirculated, so fresh outdoor air must be conditioned from the outdoor state every time.

HEPA & pressure cascades

Terminal H13 filtration and room-to-room pressure differentials add fan pressure and rule out any leak path between supply and exhaust.

Tight T & RH control

Products and processes demand stable temperature and humidity all year, so the plant heats hard in winter and cools hard in summer.

The opportunity

The exhaust stream is a heat source that is already paid for.

Air leaving the building is at room conditions. A plate heat exchanger transfers that energy to the incoming air without the two streams ever touching, and a air pump then lifts the remaining exhaust heat to a useful supply temperature.

03 — Interactive 3D simulators

Four models. Every variable is yours to change.

Orbit, zoom, explode the casing and edit the design values — airflow, recovery efficiency, outdoor and room temperatures. Every model recalculates its air states live, and any setup can be shared as a link or QR code.
